How Review Watch Works — In 3 Steps

1. You join: Claim your QR stand or buy an audit, then say YES to Review Watch on WhatsApp.

2. We watch: Every Monday we check your Google listing. If a new 1 or 2-star review appears, we write a calm, professional draft reply for you.

3. You approve: We WhatsApp you the draft. You say "post it" or "change it". Nothing goes live without you.

Example: "You got a 1-star from Thandi about waiting times. Here's a reply you can use: 'Hi Thandi, sorry about the wait, we've fixed our booking system…'" — You copy-paste, or we post it for you if you have Full Setup.
