Frequently Asked Questions

What is Review Watch? Does it auto-reply to customers?

No. Review Watch checks your Google reviews every Monday. If you receive a 1 or 2-star review, we draft a polite response and send it to you on WhatsApp. Nothing is posted without your approval.

Do you cold-message businesses about their bad reviews?

Never. We only monitor reviews for businesses that explicitly opted in—by claiming a QR stand, purchasing an audit, or booking Full Setup.

Can I cancel Review Watch anytime?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp anytime to cancel. There are no contracts or cancellation fees, and billing stops before the next Monday cycle.

Is the QR Review Stand really free, and where does it link?

Yes—complimentary for any business in South Africa with no obligation to buy anything. The QR code links directly to your Google "Write a review" prompt (not your homepage) so customers can leave feedback in seconds.

Will Full Setup guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No agency can guarantee a #1 ranking. We ensure your profile is complete, accurate, and aligned with Google's local guidelines so your business can appear properly for relevant nearby searches.

Is there a car, mall, or draw date set for the Mall Car idea?

Not yet. It is currently in the interest-testing phase. Saying you're interested in the WhatsApp community simply helps us gauge demand in your area and is not a booking or draw entry.
